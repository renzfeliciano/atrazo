2.The service is free

Atrazo is offered at no cost. There are no fees, subscriptions or charges for recording entries, backups, or using Atrazo on more than one phone.

If this ever changes, we will tell you in the app before anything is charged, and you can export your records first.

3.Your store and its recovery code

There is no sign-up. When you open a store, your phone creates a recovery code. That code is your account: it is the only way to open your store on another phone.

  • Keep your recovery code safe, on paper, away from the store. Anyone who has it can open your store.
  • We never see your recovery code and cannot reset it or look it up for you.
  • If you lose every phone with your store and also lose the recovery code, your records cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.
  • If you still have a phone with your store, you can make a new recovery code in Settings at any time.

4.Your records are yours

The clients, entries, amounts and notes you write belong to you. We do not claim ownership of them.

  • You are responsible for keeping your records accurate.
  • You are responsible for having your clients’ permission to record their names, phone numbers and balances.
  • You can export everything as a CSV file at any time from Settings.

5.Acceptable use

Use Atrazo only to keep track of credit between you and your clients. Do not use it to:

  • harass, shame or threaten anyone, including by publishing a client’s balance;
  • record information you have no right to keep;
  • break the law, or try to access another person’s store or our systems.

6.Offline use and backups

Atrazo saves every entry on your phone first, so it works without signal. When your phone is online, entries are encrypted on the phone and backed up.

Entries that are not yet backed up exist only on that phone. If the phone is lost, broken or wiped before it reconnects, those entries can be lost. The app shows how many entries are waiting to back up.

We work to keep backups safe, but we cannot guarantee that no data will ever be lost.

7.Availability and changes

Atrazo runs on free hosting and database plans. It may be slow, paused, or unavailable at times, and features may change or be removed.

Because entries are saved on your phone first, you can usually keep recording even when the service is down.

8.Ending use and deleting your store

You can stop using Atrazo at any time. You can delete your store from Settings; this removes your encrypted backups from our servers as described in the Privacy Policy.

We may remove stores that break these terms or abuse the service. Because we cannot read your records or contact you, we cannot always warn you first, so export your records regularly.
