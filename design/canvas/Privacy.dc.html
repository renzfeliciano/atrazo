1.What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. There is no sign-up, so we never ask for your name, email address or phone number. Our servers keep only:

  • a random store ID, created by your phone;
  • a one-way fingerprint of your recovery code, so a new phone can prove it has the code (the code itself cannot be worked out from it);
  • your encryption key, locked with your recovery code, so we cannot use it;
  • your phones’ names and when each was last seen, for the Phones with your store list;
  • your records, encrypted on your phone before they are sent. We cannot read client names, phone numbers, amounts or notes.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or anything you do outside Atrazo.

2.No tracking

  • No analytics, no tracking, and no ads.
  • No third-party scripts, pixels or tracking cookies.
  • Fonts and icons are bundled with the app, so opening Atrazo does not contact any other company.

3.Where it’s stored

  • On your phone, in the browser’s storage, so Atrazo works offline. This copy is readable on your phone only.
  • Encrypted backups, in a Neon Postgres database located in [DATABASE REGION].
  • The app itself is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel keeps short-lived request logs that include IP addresses.

4.Why we use it

Only to run your ledger: to back up your encrypted records and restore them on a phone that has your recovery code. We do not use anything for advertising or profiling, and we cannot, because we cannot read your records.

5.Who we share it with

We do not sell data and we do not show ads. We use only these service providers to run Atrazo:

  • Vercel, to host the app;
  • Neon, to store your encrypted backups.

If the law requires us to hand over data, we can only provide what we hold: a store ID, phone names, timestamps and encrypted records we cannot read.

6.Your clients’ information

Your records include personal information about your clients. Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you are the personal information controller for your clients’ data. Atrazo stores it only in encrypted form that we cannot read.

Ask your clients before recording their phone numbers, and only record what you need to collect what they owe.

7.Keeping and deleting data

We keep your encrypted backups for as long as your store exists. If you choose Delete store in Settings, all of its backups and phone records are removed from our servers within [NUMBER] days.

Signing out of a phone erases the Atrazo copy on that phone. Signing out a lost phone from Settings erases it the next time it connects.

If you lose every phone with your store and your recovery code, no one can open the backups. They are removed after [NUMBER] days without any phone connecting.

8.Security

  • Records are encrypted on your phone (AES-256) before they leave it, and every connection is encrypted too.
  • Only a phone with your recovery code can open your store’s backups.
  • You can see every phone that has your store, sign out any of them, and make a new recovery code.

9.Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about your store;
  • correct your records, directly in the app;
  • have it erased, by deleting your store;
  • take a copy with you, using CSV export in Settings;
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

10.Changes to this policy

When we make a significant change to this policy, we will show a notice in the app before it takes effect.

11.Contact and Data Protection Officer

For privacy questions or requests, contact our Data Protection Officer at [CONTACT EMAIL]. Because we don’t know who you are, include your store ID from Settings so we can find your store.
